SECTION 2: THE FUNNEL LINK (THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART)
————————————————————————

Every video we post needs to point people to this website address:

    https://carryon.us/get-started

BUT — we need to add special tracking tags to the end of the link so we can tell WHERE the person came from and WHICH video they watched. These tags are invisible to the viewer — they just see a normal webpage. But behind the scenes, we capture everything.

Here is how to build the link for each video on each platform:

FORMAT:
https://carryon.us/get-started?utm_source=PLATFORM&utm_medium=TYPE&utm_campaign=VIDEO_NAME

Just replace the three UPPERCASE parts:
- PLATFORM = instagram, facebook, or linkedin
- TYPE = reels, stories, feed, or sponsored
- VIDEO_NAME = a short name for the video (no spaces — use underscores)

EXAMPLES:

If you're posting Video 1 (let's say it's about protecting your family) as an Instagram Reel:
https://carryon.us/get-started?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reels&utm_campaign=video_1_protect_family

Same video, but posted on Facebook:
https://carryon.us/get-started?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=reels&utm_campaign=video_1_protect_family

Same video, but posted on LinkedIn:
https://carryon.us/get-started?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=video_1_protect_family

Video 2 (let's say it's about getting your family ready) on Instagram:
https://carryon.us/get-started?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reels&utm_campaign=video_2_family_ready

Video 3 on LinkedIn:

IMPORTANT RULES FOR THE LINKS:
- No spaces anywhere in the link. Use underscores (_) instead.
- Keep video names short and descriptive.
- Always use lowercase letters.
- Each video on each platform gets its own unique link.
- If you post the SAME video on Instagram AND Facebook, make TWO different links (one says instagram, one says facebook).
- Copy and paste the link carefully. One wrong character and the tracking won't work.
